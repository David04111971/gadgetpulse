Partner with GadgetPulse

We are building a product discovery and shopping platform featuring useful products from independent sellers, established brands and international suppliers.

Partnership paths

Affiliate programs

Brands and retailers provide approved tracking links. GadgetPulse creates product guides and promotions and earns a commission on qualifying purchases.

Supplier collaborations

Suppliers can propose products, provide accurate product information and imagery, and fulfill orders through their own approved sales channels.

Future vendor marketplace

We plan to evaluate a vendor application and listing system after payment processing, seller verification, returns, customer support and commission rules are in place.

What we look for

  • Clear product specifications, genuine photos and accurate availability.
  • Transparent pricing, shipping times, warranty and return policies.
  • Reliable fulfillment and a real process for customer support.
  • Products that offer practical value and comply with applicable laws and platform policies.
  • For international suppliers, clear landed-cost and delivery information for customers.
